Assignment 1 — Design Process

The Ultimate Animated Character Showcase

7 design variations exploring the best characters from anime and animated comedy. Each version reimagines the same 21 characters through a completely different visual lens — from Springfield's yellow glow to the Matrix's digital rain.

Design Exploration

Below are seven distinct design directions, each inspired by a different visual universe. Click any card to explore that variation. The journey goes from cartoon nostalgia to dark comic book drama to cyberpunk terminals — all built with pure HTML and CSS.

V1

Springfield Showcase

Yellow-drenched and bubbly, like Homer's beer goggles. Rounded cards, sky-blue background, donut-ring stat displays, and playful bounce animations. Pure Springfield energy.

Quahog Chronicles

Bold, brash, and unapologetic. Comic-panel grid with thick borders, bar-chart stats, and CSS card flips that reveal character details on hover. Cutaway-gag energy.

View Design → V3

Sakura Clash

Vibrant manga-panel grid with asymmetric layouts, neon gradients, speed-line backgrounds, and falling cherry blossom animations. Maximum anime protagonist energy.

View Design → V4

Gotham Files

Dark, brooding, and cinematic. Full-width comic strips, grayscale-to-color reveals, star ratings, and golden glows against a near-black canvas. Gotham after midnight.

View Design → V5

Hero Spotlight

Cinematic red-and-gold with a hero spotlight layout. Portrait-ratio cards, segmented power bars, gold shimmer titles, and radial gradient drama. Endgame-level presentation.

View Design → V6

The Construct

Green-on-black terminal aesthetic with digital rain, glitch text, CRT scanlines, and blinking cursors. Characters presented as classified dossier readouts. Take the red pill.

r/AnimeAndCartoons

Clean forum layout with upvote/downvote UI, subreddit labels, colored stat pills, and a sticky sidebar for genre filtering. Content-dense and community-flavored.
