The Definitive Gazette of Animated Character Excellence

COMPLETE CHARACTER DOSSIERS: 21 PROFILES INSIDE

Our critics have assessed the finest animated personalities across six beloved franchises. Full ratings and editorial profiles below.

ANIME DIVISION

From the Hidden Leaf Village — Naruto

Itachi Uchiha: The Tragic Mastermind

By Staff Correspondent • Naruto Bureau

I

Massacred his own clan to prevent a war, then spent years playing the villain. The original “it was all part of the plan” character.

Strength: 8/10 • Speed: 9/10 • Intelligence: 10/10 • Energy: 9/10 • Durability: 6/10

Naruto Uzumaki: From Outcast to Hokage

By Staff Correspondent • Naruto Bureau

N

Orphan jinchuriki who clawed from class clown to Seventh Hokage through sheer stubbornness. His Talk-no-Jutsu has a higher success rate than most combat techniques.

Strength: 9/10 • Speed: 8/10 • Intelligence: 6/10 • Energy: 10/10 • Durability: 9/10

Kakashi Hatake: The Copy Ninja Unmasked

By Staff Correspondent • Naruto Bureau

K

The Copy Ninja who’s read every jutsu and every volume of Make-Out Paradise. Chronically late, perpetually unbothered, and somehow always the coolest person in the room.

Strength: 7/10 • Speed: 9/10 • Intelligence: 10/10 • Energy: 7/10 • Durability: 7/10

From the Jujutsu World — Jujutsu Kaisen

Gojo Satoru: The Strongest Walks Among Us

By Senior Analyst • Jujutsu Kaisen Desk

G

The strongest sorcerer alive who treats world-ending threats like minor inconveniences. Behind the blindfold are the prettiest eyes in anime and zero humility.

Strength: 9/10 • Speed: 10/10 • Intelligence: 9/10 • Energy: 10/10 • Durability: 10/10

Sukuna: The King of Curses Reigns Supreme

By Senior Analyst • Jujutsu Kaisen Desk

S

The undisputed King of Curses who once ruled the golden age of jujutsu sorcery. Everyone else’s villain is just auditioning for his understudy.

Strength: 10/10 • Speed: 9/10 • Intelligence: 9/10 • Energy: 10/10 • Durability: 10/10

Toji Fushiguro: Zero Energy, Maximum Carnage

By Senior Analyst • Jujutsu Kaisen Desk

T

A man born with zero cursed energy who decided to become the deadliest sorcerer-killer anyway. Proof that spite is the most powerful energy system in anime.

Strength: 10/10 • Speed: 10/10 • Intelligence: 8/10 • Energy: 0/10 • Durability: 8/10

COMEDY DIVISION

Springfield Dispatch — The Simpsons

Homer Simpson: America’s Favourite Safety Inspector

By Our Springfield Correspondent

H

Safety inspector at a nuclear plant who has somehow never caused a full meltdown — debatable. Proof that you can be terrible at your job and still be a beloved cultural icon.

Humor: 9/10 • Charisma: 7/10 • Intelligence: 3/10 • Heart: 8/10 • Resilience: 10/10

Bart Simpson: Underachiever And Proud Of It

By Our Springfield Correspondent

B

Springfield’s premier underachiever and proud of it, man. Has been ten years old for over three decades and shows no signs of maturing.

Humor: 8/10 • Charisma: 10/10 • Intelligence: 6/10 • Heart: 7/10 • Resilience: 9/10

Lisa Simpson: The Conscience of a Nation

By Our Springfield Correspondent

L

An eight-year-old jazz saxophonist, Buddhist, environmentalist, and the only Simpson with a functioning moral compass. Destined to fix everything her family breaks.

Humor: 6/10 • Charisma: 3/10 • Intelligence: 10/10 • Heart: 9/10 • Resilience: 8/10

Quahog Chronicle — Family Guy

Stewie Griffin: The Infant Supervillain

By Our Quahog Bureau Chief

S

A diabolical infant with a British accent, a time machine, and unresolved mommy issues. His plans for world domination keep getting derailed by nap time.

Humor: 9/10 • Charisma: 10/10 • Intelligence: 10/10 • Heart: 5/10 • Resilience: 8/10

Peter Griffin: Hold My Beer

By Our Quahog Bureau Chief

P

A man whose entire decision-making process can be summarized as “hold my beer.” Has survived more near-death experiences than most action heroes combined.

Humor: 10/10 • Charisma: 9/10 • Intelligence: 2/10 • Heart: 6/10 • Resilience: 10/10

Quagmire: Pilot, Neighbour, Legend

By Our Quahog Bureau Chief

Q

Quahog’s most notorious neighbor whose catchphrase alone has launched a thousand memes. A Navy-trained pilot who uses his skills mainly to flee awkward situations.

Humor: 8/10 • Charisma: 8/10 • Intelligence: 5/10 • Heart: 4/10 • Resilience: 7/10

South Park Sentinel — South Park

Eric Cartman: The Sociopath Next Door

By Our Colorado Correspondent

E

A sociopathic fourth-grader who once fed a kid his own parents in chili. Somehow still gets invited to birthday parties.

Humor: 7/10 • Charisma: 10/10 • Intelligence: 8/10 • Heart: 1/10 • Resilience: 9/10

Randy Marsh: Geologist Turned Everything

By Our Colorado Correspondent

R

A geologist who has pivoted to weed farmer, pop star, and international criminal — sometimes in the same episode. The dad who makes every situation worse by trying to help.

Humor: 9/10 • Charisma: 10/10 • Intelligence: 5/10 • Heart: 6/10 • Resilience: 8/10

Butters Stotch: Innocence Under Siege

By Our Colorado Correspondent

B

The sweetest kid in South Park who keeps getting grounded for things that aren’t his fault. His alter ego Professor Chaos is about as threatening as a kitten in a cape.

Humor: 8/10 • Charisma: 3/10 • Intelligence: 4/10 • Heart: 10/10 • Resilience: 10/10

Ocean Avenue Gazette — Bob’s Burgers

Bob Belcher: The Burger Man Endures

By Our Dining Critic

B

A burger chef whose daily specials are funnier than his profit margins. Holds his chaotic family together with dad jokes and quiet, simmering desperation.

Humor: 7/10 • Charisma: 4/10 • Intelligence: 7/10 • Heart: 10/10 • Resilience: 10/10

Tina Belcher: Voice of a Generation

By Our Dining Critic

T

A thirteen-year-old whose erotic friend fiction and obsession with butts make her the most relatable awkward teen on television. Her groan is an entire emotional vocabulary.

Humor: 8/10 • Charisma: 3/10 • Intelligence: 6/10 • Heart: 9/10 • Resilience: 9/10

Louise Belcher: Tiny Terror in Bunny Ears

By Our Dining Critic

L

The youngest Belcher is a tiny chaos agent in bunny ears who could probably run a crime syndicate if she wasn’t stuck in fourth grade. Her schemes are elaborate and her mercy is nonexistent.

Humor: 7/10 • Charisma: 9/10 • Intelligence: 9/10 • Heart: 7/10 • Resilience: 8/10

Interdimensional Wire — Rick & Morty

Rick Sanchez: The Smartest Man in Any Room

By Our Interdimensional Correspondent

R

The smartest being in the multiverse who uses infinite cosmic power mostly to avoid therapy. His portal gun is less an invention and more an alcoholism enabler.

Humor: 8/10 • Charisma: 10/10 • Intelligence: 10/10 • Heart: 3/10 • Resilience: 7/10

Morty Smith: The Reluctant Adventurer

By Our Interdimensional Correspondent

M

A perpetually anxious teenager dragged through dimensions by his grandpa against his better judgment. Has seen enough horrors to fill several lifetimes but still shows up for school on Monday.

Humor: 7/10 • Charisma: 6/10 • Intelligence: 4/10 • Heart: 8/10 • Resilience: 9/10

Mr. Meeseeks: Existence Is Pain

By Our Interdimensional Correspondent

M

A being summoned into existence for a single task who experiences existential dread the longer it takes. Proof that even magical creatures hate helping people improve their golf game.

Humor: 9/10 • Charisma: 8/10 • Intelligence: 5/10 • Heart: 7/10 • Resilience: 2/10
